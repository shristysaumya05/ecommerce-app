E-Commerce Application
Product Catalog
- Product listing is available.
- Product name and price are displayed.
- Product category is displayed.
- Product availability status is displayed.

Product Search
- Products can be searched by name.
- Search results are displayed.

Pricing
- Product prices can be updated.
- Updated prices are reflected in the product catalog.

Wishlist
- Products can be added to a wishlist.
- Wishlist items can be viewed separately.

Wishlist Enhancement
- Customers can remove products from their wishlist.
- Customers can move wishlist products from the shopping cart directly to checkout.

Customer Support
- Customers can view support information.
- Support requests can be submitted.

Order Tracking
- Customers can view order status.
- Order status is displayed using standardized status values.
- Order status is updated as the order progresses.

Payment Processing
- Customers can complete payments securely.
- Payment status is recorded for each order.

Order Management
- Customers can view their orders.
- Customers can review previous order details.

Customer Profile
- Customers can view their profile information.
- Customers can update their profile information.

Order History
- Customers can view their previous orders.
- Customers can review the details of completed orders.

Delivery Management
- Customers can view delivery information.
- Delivery status is updated as orders progress.

Payment Timeout Handling
- Payment requests are monitored for timeout conditions.
- Timed-out payments are marked for retry.

Payment Timeout Hotfix
- Timed-out payments are clearly identified.
- Customers receive a retry option when payment processing times out.

Discount Management
- Customers can apply promotional discounts.
- Discount eligibility is validated before checkout.
- Customers can view the discount applied to their order.

Order Cancellation
- Customers can cancel eligible orders.
- Cancellation status is recorded for the order.

Order Reports
- Order reports can be generated.
- Order information can be summarized for review.

Customer Reviews
- Customers can submit reviews for purchased products.
- Customers can view reviews submitted by other customers.
